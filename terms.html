Prototype terms

Terms summary

Loop is a local prototype for exploring a short-video app idea. It is not a hosted social network. Your uploaded media and activity remain in your browser on this device.

Content you choose

Only use videos and sounds that you created or have permission to use. You remain responsible for the files you select. Do not use the prototype to threaten or target people, violate their privacy, or share illegal or abusive material.

Limits of this preview

There are no live user accounts, remote moderation, cloud backups, real-time messages, live broadcasts, or licensed music catalogue. Demo actions such as reporting, blocking, and messaging are local interface previews. Availability of your browser storage is controlled by your browser and device.
