Community guidelines

Loop is for creative short videos and friendly conversation. Treat people with respect and only post material you made or have permission to share.

Keep people safe

  • No threats, harassment, bullying, doxxing, or targeted abuse.
  • No sexual exploitation or content involving the abuse of children.
  • No dangerous instructions or encouragement to harm yourself or others.
  • No impersonation, scams, spam, or misleading links.
  • Respect other people's privacy, copyright, and consent.

Using report and block

The prototype includes report and block controls for demonstration. Reports are kept on your device and are not sent to a moderator. Blocking hides that creator in your local feed only. A public community needs a real moderation team and server-backed report, block, appeal, and response workflows.

Help

There is no live support service attached to this preview. Add a working support contact and moderation process before inviting the public to post content.
