Privacy summary

Loop currently runs as a local browser prototype. It does not create online accounts or send videos, profile details, likes, comments, messages, or reports to a Loop server.

What stays on this device

Profile settings, likes, follows, saved loops, comments, demo messages, and preferences are stored in this browser's local storage. Uploaded videos and saved drafts are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. Other people cannot see or receive them through this prototype.

Sharing and device storage

If you choose a browser share action, your browser may share the current page link using its own sharing interface. You can remove local app data using Profile → Settings → Delete local data and reset, or by clearing this site's browser storage. Removing the app or clearing browser data may also remove saved videos and drafts. A hosting provider may separately process basic connection logs; check its terms before launch.

Before a public social launch

A public service needs a final privacy policy describing its actual hosting, account, analytics, storage, retention, deletion, and support practices. This page is a prototype summary, not a substitute for that policy.
